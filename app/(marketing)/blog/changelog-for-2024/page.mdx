## Powering Service Businesses Across Every Industry

At ACAI, we don't believe in one-size-fits-all automation. Every service business operates differently, and our AI systems are built to match that reality.

From local shops to growing franchises, ACAI helps service companies capture more calls, book more appointments, and deliver better customer experiences.

## Built for Every Service Industry

### Auto Detailing

We automate appointment scheduling, follow-ups, and service confirmations so detailers can stay focused on delivering high-quality work. ACAI handles customer inquiries, package selections, and booking reminders, ensuring no detail is overlooked.

### Barbershops and Salons

ACAI manages bookings, reschedules, and client inquiries in real time, reducing no-shows and keeping chairs full. Our system integrates seamlessly with scheduling platforms and sends automated reminders to maximize utilization.

### Cleaning Companies

Our system handles recurring bookings, service reminders, and quote requests, helping cleaning teams scale without added staff. From residential to commercial cleaning, ACAI adapts to your service offerings and client communication style.

### Plumbing and HVAC

We qualify urgent calls, route emergency requests, and book service visits instantly, ensuring no lead is missed. ACAI understands the difference between routine maintenance and emergencies, routing calls appropriately and capturing critical customer information.

Each system is customized to match the workflow of the business it supports.
