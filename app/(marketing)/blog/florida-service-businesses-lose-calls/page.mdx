## The 60% Problem

Industry data shows that service businesses miss **up to 60% of inbound calls** during peak hours. For small teams, it is even worse. You are on a job. Your hands are dirty. You cannot stop what you are doing to answer the phone.

But here is the thing the customer does not care about:

- They do not care you are busy
- They do not care you will call back
- They already called your competitor

**If you miss 3 calls a day, that is 3 jobs gone. Simple as that.**

## Why Voicemail Does Not Work

Nobody leaves voicemails anymore. Especially not homeowners looking for a quick service. They call the next number on Google and move on.

Here is what a typical missed call looks like:

1. Customer searches "AC repair near me"
2. Clicks your ad (you just paid $15-40 for that click)
3. Calls your number
4. No answer
5. Calls your competitor
6. Books with them

Your ad budget just funded your competitor's next job.
