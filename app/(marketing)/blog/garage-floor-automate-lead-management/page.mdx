Running a garage floor business in Florida means your leads come in fast. Someone wants their garage done. They want it done soon. They are calling 3-4 companies and going with whoever gets back to them first.

**If your lead management is a spreadsheet and a stack of missed calls, you are bleeding money.**

I have spoken with garage floor companies in Tampa, Brandon, Riverview, and across the bay area. The ones that are growing fast all have one thing in common — they respond to every lead within minutes, not hours.

## The Lead Management Problem

Most garage floor companies manage leads like this:

- Leads come in from Google Ads, Facebook, or a website form
- They sit in an inbox or a CRM nobody checks during the day
- Someone follows up in the evening or the next morning
- By then, the customer has already booked with a competitor

This is not a lead quality problem. This is a **lead management problem**.

## What Automation Actually Looks Like

When we talk about automating lead management, we are not talking about some complicated CRM with 50 dashboards. We are talking about a simple system that does three things:

1. **Responds instantly** — text, call, or both within 60 seconds
2. **Qualifies automatically** — asks about garage size, coating type, timeline, budget
3. **Books the appointment** — puts it on your calendar without you touching anything

That is it. No complex setup. No IT team. Just a system that catches every lead the moment it comes in.
